## Collaboration

During our first meeting, my team and I had chosen to do a simple but fun project, which is a text highlighter extension with stretch goals for bookmarking or pinning the text for future reference. We took quite some time thinking about an idea for our project since none of us had experience building a chrome extension. However, we decided that this project would be doable and not too complicated based on our research. It hasn't been too hard getting everyone on the same page. We haven't faced too many problems yet as we are still in the initial stages of the project. Some good things that have happened are that our group has diverse skills, such as 3D animation in Blender, JavaScript, and Git experience. I fit into the collaboration by suggesting ideas for approaching the project, sharing resources for documentation and tutorials, suggesting how we could delegate work, listening to others suggestions, and working together towards a unified goal. My biggest contributions have been in the discussions, initiating ideas, creating the repositories, and submitting the responses for the previous class' project activity. I will be working on implementing the chrome extension, building out the core and extra features of the extension. I was surprised by how I'm able to take things head on even without fully knowing how to do something or having experience with it. I think that's a positive trait to be able to do things outside my comfort zone and keep growing in my soft/hard skills.
