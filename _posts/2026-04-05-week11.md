## Cathedral vs Bazaar + Group Project Update

We discussed the cathedral vs bazaar model of development by Eric S. Raymond (ESR) this week. It was interesting to see how Linux's style of development completely overturned how ESR approached development and how it changed the industry moving forward. You could build things with more agility, flexibility, and speed due to its open source nature. Releasing early and often is a core part of building a bazaar. Hence, with AI tools writing code, we get to see this happen more efficiently and quickly. However, there is a tradeoff. I'm seeing patterns where developers trade ease of use and speed over care for craft and clean/understandable/maintainable code. <!--more-->

Using AI tools to write code is "easy" but not necessarily the "best" approach. Part of the bazaar approach is to listen to customers, understand the nuances of their issues, discuss tradeoffs with teammates, and to be curious about issues that happen and why they happen. Those are the fun parts of development. With AI tools writing code, it's possible that we might trade those human-to-human connections for fast, easy AI solutions. In my opinion, using AI as a way to code is aligned with the bazaar model of releasing early and often, but planning, discussing, and being curious before even coding is crucial as a software engineer to ensure the approach chosen to solve the problem is the best. If AI produces slop and you aren't able to verify that as an engineer, then releasing early and often may no longer be the case as you'll be stuck figuring out what AI has written and how to fix it.

In terms of the group project, our group has made great progress in each of our PRs. We've successfully fixed a few UI issues and merged PRs. Other groups have also had success in terms of merging their PRs. There were some groups who had access to documentation changes and "beginner" contributor workshops. However, that is not the case with my group's project. Nevertheless, not having those resources has pushed us to dive into the codebase and community more deeply and understand it better as a contributor and a user. In the coming weeks, I hope to accomplish making bigger changes to the codebase by finding backend or non-web platforms to have a more complete understanding of the codebase. In the process of doing that, I hope to engage more with the community and have more discussions in the PR thread. I also hope to help my team members with their PRs and contributions when I can.
