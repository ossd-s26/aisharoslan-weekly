## Group Work
So far, our group work has been great. Every team member has been active in discussions, in voicing out their needs and concerns in the working agreement, suggesting ideas, and asking for help. For the project we chose, we first asked each other if we had any projects that we each wanted to work on. We also discussed our strengths and interests and what we might like to work on. Most of us have frontend/fullstack experience and so, we made sure the project we chose allowed the opportunity to use those skills. Once we all laid out the potential projects, we did research on our own and came back during the next class to make a decision. We all voted and decided on AFFiNE, a Notion/Miro alternative. There were no strong oppositions in choosing this project and everyone had agreed to it out of all our options. We hit obstacles in setting up/installing the project during class, but some of us were able to resolve the issues and help each other out. <!--more--> Another obstacle I think is going to be the fact that we're not currently users of the product, and so, we'd all need to start getting familiar with the product as users. We've all used Notion/Google Docs/something similar. However, learning the unique features of AFFiNE will be crucial in contributions. Based on the project evaluation I made, there is also a very small number of project maintainers which may be an obstacle timeline wise. However, the maintainer is very active and responsive and keeps the project progressing forward. As a group, we will help each other overcome such obstacles by reaching out to the maintainers and keeping an eye out for each other. Here's our [group wiki](https://github.com/ossd-s26/wiki/wiki/AFFiNE-(Group-D)).
