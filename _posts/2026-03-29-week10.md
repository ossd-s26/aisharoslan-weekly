## Group Project Update

Midterm reflections were done this week. I enjoyed having that moment to just reflect on my experiences in open source over the past few weeks, such as the lectures and discussions that have exposed me to different aspects of open source and the group work experiences I've had throughout this class. 

I've also officially contributed to my group project, AFFiNE, this week! It took me a while, but I found an easy enough, beginner-friendly [issue](https://github.com/toeverything/AFFiNE/issues/14528) to work on. <!--more--> There's such a variety of issue types for AFFiNE, so being able to find this issue that has such detailed information on the problem and the solution really helped me understand the codebase better and how things work in relation to this issue. My process was: recreate issue, attempt a fix, test/verify, and wait for review. Initially, I didn't realize there was a proposed solution in the issue description. Hence, I iterated on my original solution and improved it to the proposed solution to remove browser dependency completely.

I was also really surprised to find that my PR merged within 1 day! It was a small fix, but still! I saw it being included in a release on March 31, but have not seen the fix reflected yet on the website (will check-in again!). In terms of blockers, I did have issues getting responses on the documentation repo. I tried looking for issues, creating a discussion post, and asking in Discord about it, but to no avail :( Discussions and Discord are pretty active, but only if people in the community are excited about it. Anyways, I'm looking forward to finding other kinds of issues (non-UI and non-web) to develop a deeper understanding of the app and the codebase!
